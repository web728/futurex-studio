# Futurex Studio owner handover

## What is complete

The approved website design, responsive pages, portfolio, project pages, contact flow, legal drafts, SEO structure, sitemap, robots controls and secure form architecture are prepared for developer deployment. A clean developer package and setup instructions are included.

## What the developer must configure

- Final domain, DNS and HTTPS
- MongoDB, the existing Google Sheet, Resend email, reCAPTCHA and Upstash
- Preview versus Production indexing settings
- Approved old-site redirects
- A real end-to-end form test, monitoring and launch rollback access

No passwords or provider keys are included in the handover.

## What the owner must confirm

- Final phone, email, WhatsApp and business details
- Every project fact, testimonial and claim
- Permission to publish every image, logo, project and testimonial
- Final domain and access ownership
- Privacy Policy and Terms with qualified legal advice
- Both form-notification recipients
- Whether/when analytics and consent should be enabled

Two project hero images are only 960×540 and verified same-project gallery choices remain limited. External provider credentials, final DNS and a real production form test are still pending.

## Before launch

Review `CONTENT-VERIFICATION.md`, `ASSUMPTIONS.md`, `IMAGE-USAGE-AUDIT.md`, the legal pages, `REDIRECT-PLAN.md` and `DEVELOPER-HANDOVER.md`. Test the site on real phones and Safari/iPhone as well as desktop. Submit the form and confirm the database record, Sheet row and both emails.

Do not change the approved layout, wording, motion, image assignments, form architecture or SEO without approval. If the new release must be reversed, the developer can redeploy stable commit `21aa49f`; this does not undo form data already received.
